About MARS

An Iraqi firm, rooted in the Kurdistan Region.

Established in 2007 in the Kurdistan Region of Iraq, MARS is an Iraqi firm with a broad presence in the oil and petroleum products sector. The firm’s activities span oil transportation and distribution, the import and export of petroleum products, and the operation of a chain of fuel stations.

With experience across multiple areas of the petroleum supply chain, MARS is involved in oil projects throughout the Kurdistan Region and the rest of Iraq. Its work connects the transportation and movement of oil products with their distribution and availability to the market. Through its diverse activities, the firm serves commercial partners and customers across Iraq’s energy sector.

Since its establishment, MARS has continued to build its role in Iraq’s oil industry, combining its regional roots with operations that extend across the country.

The firm’s main work is buying and selling in the petroleum sector. It runs fuel-station chains in Sulaymaniyah, operates its own storage station, and works both inside Iraq and outside it.

Oil terminals

Fuel is received in bulk, kept in storage tanks by product, and loaded through a rack into road tankers for stations, customers, and export. MARS does this alongside the storage station it already runs, inside Iraq and outside it.

Governmental contracts

A public assignment to supply fuel on fixed terms: the product, the quantity, the places it must reach, and the quality it must meet. MARS carries that through, from sourcing and transport to handover, across the Kurdistan Region and the rest of Iraq.

Logistical contracts

The agreement that moves petroleum products from where they are held to where they are needed: the schedule, tanker transport, time in storage, and delivery. For MARS this joins import and export with the Sulaymaniyah station chains and with work outside Iraq.
